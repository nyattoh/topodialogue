## Description

TopoDialogue IV introduces a modality-provenance event contract and deterministic audit for measuring which observable interaction events and features are preserved, omitted, or falsely generated when speech, direct text, and AAC pathways are normalised into dialogue records. Five constructed cases validate arithmetic, missingness reasons, correction history, and privacy-preserving local references. The study makes no field-accuracy, diagnostic, emotional, disability, or cross-cultural-validity claim; human agreement and participatory review remain explicit next gates. Includes a British-English PDF, Japanese companion text, source, tests, figures, and constructed fixtures.
